proposal (`Methodology_Proposal_to_7Dec.docx`, "prop §") and the Data Summary (`Data_Summary_to_7Dec.docx`,
"DS §11"), done once as remaining-work plan step 10. Each row gives a requirement, the artefact that meets
it, how it is checked, and its status:

- **done**: built and checked;
- **partial**: built for the first set or as tooling, with the rest waiting for something named in the row;
- **team**: waits for a team decision or team work;
