## Expert review (H1b)

Every GNU Radio question and every Tier-F and Tier-A task, reviewed by the expert agent (`docs/checklists/expert_review.md`). H1b holds for a tier when at least 90 % of its reviewed items are accepted (first pass; the items the two-reviewer review removed count too); this is the expert agent's acceptance, not a GNU Radio engineer's (pre-registration §10). H1b counts the original pool of items; the tasks that replace the rejected ones are counted beside it. The columns after 'H1b holds' describe; they are not the test.
