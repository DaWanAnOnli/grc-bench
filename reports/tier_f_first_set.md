## 3. Split discipline

Signature v2 describes a flowgraph by the set of functions its blocks perform, so reordering blocks or adding an inert block (a Throttle) does not change it (`reports/tier_f_signature_v2.md`). A composed task's split must equal the split its reference cell hashes to. An example task's split comes from its example file, so its cell may hash to any split; that cell is reserved like every other test and dev cell.

- Tasks whose split disagrees with the rule: **0**.
- Groups of tasks that share a reference cell across splits: **0**.
- Reserved cells (reference and variant cells of test and dev tasks, plus the start and golden end-state cells of the Tier-A tasks, kept out of training data): **61** (31 hash to test, 12 to dev, 18 to train). The training team excludes all of them.

## 4. Gates

`./gb tier-f gates all` runs five gates on every task:
- **solvability**: the reference passes V1–V5 in 10 of 10 runs, 8 at a time;
- **size**: the reference fits in 3,000 tokens;
- **variants**: other valid solutions pass V5 (at least two);
- **start state**: a modify, repair or parameter-tune task's start graph fails;
- **mutation**: at least 90 % of the broken versions of the reference fail V5.
