## Method and thresholds

| Signal | Unit | Flag when | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8-gram | whole documents | the item shares ≥ 1 word 8-gram with a document (coverage ≥ 50% of the item's 8-grams also reported) | lexical, exact |
| TF-IDF cosine | passages | max cosine ≥ 0.55 | **lexical** (not paraphrase detection) |
| Embedding cosine | passages | max cosine ≥ 0.88 | semantic |

- Tokenizer (all lexical signals): lowercase, every non-word character (`\W`) to a space, split on whitespace. With it, 26 of the 185 frozen test questions share an 8-gram with the 1,668 training questions (eval plan §6), reproduced by `tests/test_contamination.py`.
- Passages: a document of at most 80 words is one passage; longer documents are cut into 64-word windows every 32 words, so a question repeated inside a long answer or session is still visible to the cosine signals.
- TF-IDF: w = (1 + ln tf)·(ln((1 + N)/(1 + df)) + 1), df over the corpus passages, rows L2-normalised (the weighting of `grcbench.splits.twins`). The threshold is that module's lexical threshold: in its reading of training pairs, the lowest band in which at least half of the pairs were twins.
- Embeddings: EmbeddingGemma 300M (QAT Q8_0 GGUF, SHA-256 `6fa0c02a9c302be6…`, the file GRC-Agent pins) on llama.cpp b10333 `llama-server --embeddings --pooling mean`, context 2048, prompt `task: sentence similarity | query: ` on both sides, vectors L2-normalised. Training questions and task specs are embedded completely. For the long corpora only the best passage of each item's 3 (default) nearest TF-IDF documents is embedded (≈15M words do not fit a CPU session), so there the embedding maximum is a lower bound and a paraphrase with little word overlap can be missed.
- The embedding threshold 0.88 comes from the training-side calibration below (no test item was used to set it): it lies above every random training-question pair (max 0.828) and below every pair adjudicated in the dataset's dedupe step (min 0.937). Both thresholds are Claude's reading of 14 Oct 2026, pending team review.
- Dev questions are excluded from all training use, so the training questions corpus drops them and the answer and repair corpora drop the rows of dev questions.

## Limits

- Flags are candidates. Template twins (the same flowgraph question for a different signal) share 8-grams and score high on both cosines without being the same question; the review sheet separates them.
- The embedding maximum over the long corpora is a lower bound (TF-IDF candidates only); `--embed-topk` widens it.
- Task-level overlap for agent tasks (same goal or same starting flowgraph, eval plan §6) needs the task metadata of Tier-A items and is checked when those items exist.
